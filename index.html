Given data

Absolute P in kPa; T in K; total V in m³; mass in kg. Fixed mass at both states.

Method reference

PV = mRT; v = V/m.
R = R̄/M (Table A–1).

At constant pressure: T₂/T₁ = V₂/V₁.

Tavg = (T₁ + T₂)/2.
Δu = cv ΔT; Δh = cp ΔT.
ΔU = m Δu; ΔH = m Δh.

Air: Δu = u₂ − u₁, Δh = h₂ − h₁ (A–22, kJ/kg).

Other gases: divide A–23 molar property differences by M (kg/kmol).
